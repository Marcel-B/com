Someone raided the biscuit tin at the office. Krümelspur is a logic puzzle on a grid: from your colleagues' statements
you work out who stood where and who took the biscuits. The app is in German for now.

## The rules

- Every row and every column holds exactly one figure. One of the figures is the biscuit tin.
- Nobody can stand on furniture, except on chairs.
- Every figure makes a true statement, such as “I was in the copy room” or “I stood right next to a plant”.
- The thief was alone with the tin in one room.

## How to play

- **Pick a tool:** tap a cross, the eraser or a figure at the bottom, then tap a cell. Press and hold a cell to see every
  option.
- **Find rooms:** tapping a room in the legend highlights it on the grid.
- **Accuse:** once you know who did it, choose them under “Anklage” (accusation) and tap “Lösung prüfen” (check
  solution).
- **New puzzle:** through the plus at the top, with size (6×6 or 8×8) and difficulty (easy, medium, hard).

## Common questions

**I'm stuck.** Tap the light bulb. The hint shows the next safe step, marks the cell and explains it step by step. If you
made a mistake earlier, it tells you that first.

**Do I have to guess?** No. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be worked out without guessing. On easy, simple
conclusions are enough; hard also needs assumptions that lead to a contradiction.

**I want to see the solution.** “Auflösen” (reveal) shows it; “Nächster Fall” (next case) moves on.

**Does the app need the internet or an account?** No. It runs entirely on the device and collects no data, see the
[privacy policy](/en/datenschutz-kruemelspur.html).
