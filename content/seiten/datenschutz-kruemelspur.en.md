This is a translation; the [German privacy policy](/datenschutz-kruemelspur.html) is the binding one.

## In short

Krümelspur is a logic puzzle that runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad. The app collects no data about you, needs no
account and no internet connection, and sends nothing to me, the developer, or to third parties: no analytics, no
advertising, no crash reports, no third-party libraries. It asks for no permission, neither camera, photos, location nor
notifications.

## Controller

Marcel Benders, address in the [legal notice](/en/impressum.html).

## What the app processes

- **Puzzles:** The app creates every puzzle on the device. Nothing is transmitted.
- **Your input:** Figures, crosses and your accusation are only kept while the app runs. There is no account, no game
  state on a server and no leaderboard.

## What Apple learns

Downloading and updating the app go through the App Store, test versions through TestFlight; for those,
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/) applies. If you agreed in the iOS settings to share
analytics with app developers, Apple gives me anonymous crash reports and usage figures, without anything that points to
you.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, to object, and to complain to a data
protection authority, in North Rhine-Westphalia the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
Since I receive no data from the app, there is nothing of yours with me to disclose or delete; delete the app and
everything is gone. I am happy to answer questions:
[marcel.benders@b-velop.com](mailto:marcel.benders@b-velop.com).
