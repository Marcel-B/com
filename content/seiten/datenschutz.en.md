## In short

This site sets no cookies, counts no visitors and loads nothing from third parties, not even fonts: the Ubuntu font lives
on the same server as the site. There is no contact form and no account. Your choice of colour scheme is stored only in
your own browser (local storage) and never sent anywhere.

## Controller

Marcel Benders, address in the [legal notice](/en/impressum.html).

## Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.
When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time, the page requested
and your browser, in order to deliver the site and protect it against attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; the
legitimate interest is a secure, available website. GitHub may process this data in the USA; GitHub is certified under the
EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details are in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Links to other sites

The projects' source code is on github.com, and some projects have sites of their own. Following a link there takes you
off this site; the privacy policy of that provider then applies.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your data, to object, and to lodge
a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in North Rhine-Westphalia the State Commissioner for Data
Protection and Freedom of Information. As this site itself stores no data, please contact GitHub about server logs, or me
([marcel.benders@b-velop.com](mailto:marcel.benders@b-velop.com)).
