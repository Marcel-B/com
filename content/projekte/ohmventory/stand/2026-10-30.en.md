In a few days Ohmventory has grown from a skeleton into a tool that works day to day. For photo recognition it now uses a
larger model that reads small markings on breakout boards more reliably; the project assistant stays with the small, fast
model.

If you want to try Ohmventory yourself, there is a [guide for the Mac](/en/projekte/ohmventory/mac-einrichten.html).
