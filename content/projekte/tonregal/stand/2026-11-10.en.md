Searching, rating and playlists now work in daily use without needing Navidrome for them; it only counts plays in the
background and gets a copy of the stars for its own lists.

Next comes the tag editor. Until now a misspelt artist or a missing year could only be fixed with another program working
on the files directly. Soon this happens on the album page, for one track or the whole album, including your own cover.
Because the files are the truth in Tonregal, every change goes into the file itself, and the file moves to its new place in
the folder structure. Stars, playlists and lyrics go along.
