---
titel: From model to figure on the shelf
zusammenfassung: 3d-o-mat is learning to export models for 3D printers and to put figures into a nice pose first; built and tested with sample figures, not yet with its own models.
fertig:
  - Upload pictures and cut them out automatically
  - Have pictures checked by Gemma
  - Have pictures painted from an idea, with three image models to choose from
  - Turn them into a textured 3D model with TRELLIS.2, view it in the browser and download it as GLB
  - Add a skeleton, with test moves in the browser and as animations in the GLB
  - New versions install themselves as soon as no job is running
  - The interface in English, with a switch and the browser's language as the default
  - Colour scheme light, dark or following the system, selectable and remembered
in_arbeit:
  - Print file as 3MF or STL, watertight, with a base and a height you choose
  - Ready-made poses such as “Relaxed” or “Wave”, fine-tuning bones by hand
  - Describe a pose, and Gemma poses the figure
geplant:
  - Server messages and error texts in English too
  - Colour print files for printers with several filaments
  - A warning about parts too thin to print
---

A figure in a T-pose is handy for animation, but not a nice model for the shelf. So 3d-o-mat is getting a way to the
3D printer: you can put the figure into a pose, with ready-made poses, with sliders for every bone or with a sentence
like “standing relaxed, right hand waving” that Gemma turns into joint angles. Then the pose is baked into the mesh,
everything is merged into one watertight body and placed on a base. The result is a 3MF file for common slicers.

Only the shape is printed: whatever the model only painted into its texture, such as eyes or patterns, is missing from
a single-colour print. The process has been tested with sample figures; models made with 3d-o-mat are next.
