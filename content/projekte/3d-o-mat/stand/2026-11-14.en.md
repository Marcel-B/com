titel: Importing your own figures
zusammenfassung: A finished figure from Blender, Mixamo or a shop now comes into 3d-o-mat as GLB, glTF or OBJ and can then do everything a model made from a picture can; tried with sample figures, not yet on the Mac.
fertig:
  - Upload pictures and cut them out automatically
  - Have pictures checked by Gemma
  - Have pictures painted from an idea, with three image models to choose from
  - Turn them into a textured 3D model with TRELLIS.2, view it in the browser and download it as GLB
  - Add a skeleton, with test moves in the browser and as animations in the GLB
  - New versions install themselves as soon as no job is running
  - The interface in English, with a switch and the browser's language as the default
  - Colour scheme light, dark or following the system, selectable and remembered
  - Print file as 3MF or STL, watertight, with a base and a height you choose
  - Ready-made poses such as “Relaxed” or “Wave”, fine-tuning bones and fists by hand
  - Describe a pose, and Gemma poses the figure
  - Test moves with more punch, a closed fist when striking, a roundhouse kick and a fall with gravity
in_arbeit:
  - 'Motion library: 244 recordings from two free collections carried over to your own figure, with speed, start and
    end, in place and mirror, up to twelve as animations in the GLB'
  - 'Cleaner motions: feet that stay planted instead of sliding when walking, loops without a jump, more space between
    arms and body, root motion for Godot and an upright posture instead of looking up and standing on the heels'
  - 'Fingers: the finger bones created when the skeleton is inserted get names, so the fist, poses and Godot know
    them; in the library each recording can run with closed hands'
  - 'Joint editor: drag a joint that sits wrong into place by hand, mirror it, save it and bring back the original'
  - A message through [Onkel Bummel](/en/projekte/onkel-bummel/) when a model, a skeleton or a painted picture is done or fails, with a preview picture
  - 'An interface for other programs: order a GLB in a fixed shape from a picture, for example for a game that turns
    every player''s picture into a head'
  - 'Import your own figure: GLB, glTF or OBJ with textures, one by one, together or as a ZIP; a figure with a Mixamo
    skeleton goes straight to the motion library, any other gets its skeleton from SkinTokens'
  - Server messages and error texts in English too

---

Until now every figure in 3d-o-mat started as a picture. If you already had a finished figure, from Blender, from
Mixamo, from an asset shop or as a GLB from img-o-mat, there was no way to bring it in. Now
there is a field for it: drop the file, and the figure is in the list right away, without the Mac having to compute
anything.

GLB, glTF and OBJ are accepted. Whatever belongs to a figure, such as an OBJ's material file and textures, goes along
at once or as a ZIP. 3d-o-mat turns it into a GLB like one made from a picture: colours and textures come along,
missing surface normals are computed, and texture formats glTF does not know become PNG. If a texture is missing, the
figure comes without it and the page says which one.

After that the figure can do everything a model made from a picture can: turn, download, become a print file and get a
skeleton. If it already has a skeleton with Mixamo names, even that step falls away; it opens right away with the
motion library, poses and the joint editor. This has been tried with freely available sample figures in the browser,
among them two with a Mixamo skeleton that ran recordings from the library straight away and an OBJ figure with three
textures. Trying it with your own figures on the Mac is still to come. FBX will follow later; until then, export a GLB
from Blender.
